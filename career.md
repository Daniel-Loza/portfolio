# Career Goals

Looking forward, I am hoping to set foot into the semiconductor device fabrication industry. To achieve this, I am planning to take advantage of a variety of resources at Georgia Tech.

In my undergraduate studies, I will likely commit to the electronic devices and circuit technology electrical engineering threads. This coursework will give me a strong foundation in necessary industry skills.
In order to gain research and cleanroom experience early, I will participate in the Chip Scale and Power VIP (Vertically Integrated Project) next semester, where I will research carbon nanotube technology.
Additionally, I am planning to take advantage of Georgia Tech's BS/MS program to gain even stronger foundational knowledge.

Outside of coursework, I will continue to gain hands-on skills and engineering experience through clubs. I will engineer equipment and fabrication processes through the Nanotech Club and explore VLSI/physical design through SiliconJackets.
In order to gain a broader understanding of the electrical engineering field, I will finally also contribute to Georgia Tech Solar Racing for a semester.

I hope that all of these experiences will allow me to succeed in future semiconductor device fabrication internships and full-time roles.
